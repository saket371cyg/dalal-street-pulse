Chapter 1: Project Overview & Problem Statement

The Indian equity market is among the world's most dynamic financial ecosystems, with over 160+ million Demat accounts across CDSL and NSDL. Daily trading turnover consistently exceeds ₹1 Lakh Crore in cash equities and ₹300+ Lakh Crore in derivatives across the National Stock Exchange (NSE) and BSE Ltd.

However, existing market analytics tools remain foreign-centric: they display prices using Western millions/billions rather than Lakhs/Crores, ignore official NSE trading session schedules (Pre-Open 09:00–09:15, Block Trading, and Indian holidays), and fail to reconcile dual-listed stocks (NSE tickers vs. BSE Scrip codes).

Deficiencies in Existing Tools Industry Gaps
  • Western Number Formatting: Displays ₹1,000,000 instead of ₹10,00,000; lacks Market Cap in Lakh/Crores.
  • Unsynchronized Sessions: Ignores NSE Pre-Open (09:00–09:15) and post-market trading windows.
  • Ticker Disconnection: Inability to correlate NSE tickers (`RELIANCE`) with BSE codes (`500325`).
  • Hallucinatory AI: Generates generic commentary detached from audited financial statements.
Dalal Street Pulse Innovations Engineered Solutions
  • Authentic INR Localization: Custom algorithms displaying ₹15.42 Lakh Cr and ₹12,34,567.89 accurately.
  • State-Aware IST Clock: Real-time session detection: Pre-open, Regular, Post-closing, and Holidays.
  • ISIN Universal Linkage: Universal entity resolution linking NSE, BSE, and SME instruments.
  • Grounded Valuation: Rule-based and Gemini AI evaluation computing fundamental health scores (1–10).

Chapter 3: Market Data Engine & ISIN Reconciliation

A central architectural breakthrough of Dalal Street Pulse is its **ISIN Canonical Reconciler**. Dual-listed companies in India possess distinct symbols on the NSE (e.g. RELIANCE) and BSE (e.g. Scrip Code 500325). To prevent duplicate tickers, data divergence, or fake quotes, the platform unifies all instruments by their 12-character International Securities Identification Number.

Ingested Security Masters Official Sources
  • NSE Mainboard: EQUITY_L.csv (2,240+ equities with Face Value, Listing Date, Series).
  • BSE Mainboard: BSE_Directory.csv (4,800+ listed scrips across Groups A, B, T).
  • SME Platforms: SME_L.csv for NSE EMERGE and BSE SME counters.
  • Symbol Renames: Official NSE symbol_change.csv mapped to aliases.
Pluggable Provider Routing Multi-Tier Fallback
  • TrueData Adapter: Low-latency L1 streaming ticks (<50ms) for trading hours.
  • EODHD Adapter: 10+ years of audited quarterly financial statements and candles.
  • Hybrid Router: Evaluates provider health dynamically; routes requests to optimal endpoint with cache fallback.

Chapter 5: Database Architecture & Storage Models

The platform's data layer is designed around high-throughput MongoDB collections with compound indexes, optimized for low-latency queries and atomic watchlist updates.

Collection Key Fields / Schema Indexes & Performance Role
instruments isin, nseSymbol, bseCode, companyName, isSME, sector Unique index on isin; sparse indexes on nseSymbol and bseCode. Rapid entity lookup.
watchlists userId, name, symbols[], createdAt Compound index on { userId: 1, name: 1 }. Strict user data isolation.
alerts userId, symbol, targetPrice, condition, status Index on { status: 1, symbol: 1 }. Fast retrieval during Inngest batch evaluation.
daily_briefings date, headline, niftySummary, keyCatalysts[] Index on date (descending). Instant SSR rendering of post-market reports.
financial_statements symbol, period, statementType, values{} Compound index on { symbol: 1, period: -1, statementType: 1 }.

5.1 Resilient In-Memory Development Store

To ensure zero-friction onboarding and continuous offline operation, lib/db/store.ts provides a high-fidelity in-memory persistence store. If MONGODB_URI is unavailable, the application gracefully routes all mutations, alert states, and briefings to this local store without crashes.

Chapter 6: Automated Workflows & Inngest Engine

Workflow Name Trigger Schedule Operational Purpose
security-master-sync Daily at 06:00 IST (Cron) Ingests updated NSE/BSE security masters, tracks new listings, and maps symbol changes prior to market open.

generate-daily-briefing Daily at 16:30 IST (Post-Close) Aggregates day's trading statistics, calculates sector leaders/laggards, calls Gemini AI, and archives post-market brief.

Chapter 8: Strategic Future Scope & Roadmap

Dalal Street Pulse is positioned to transition from an analytics and intelligence platform into a comprehensive **Execution, Derivatives & Wealth Management Operating System** for the Indian market.

Phase 1 1-Click Broker Trading & Execution Gateway
Direct connectivity with SEBI-registered retail stockbrokers via secure OAuth integrations.
  • Broker Adapters: Seamless integrations with Zerodha (Kite Connect), Groww, Upstox, Angel One (SmartAPI), and Dhan HQ.
  • Native Order Routing: Place CNC (Long-term), MIS (Intraday), and GTT (Good Till Triggered) orders directly from the stock details terminal.
  • Paper Trading Simulator: Virtual ₹10 Lakh INR demo portfolio with real-time exchange matching for risk-free strategy validation.
  • Thematic Basket Orders: Single-click execution of curated stock baskets (e.g. "NIFTY Auto Momentum", "High Dividend PSUs").
Phase 2 NSE Derivatives (F&O) & Options Analytics Terminal
Professional-grade intelligence for index and equity options traders.
  • Interactive Option Chain: Live Open Interest (OI), Change in OI, Put-Call Ratio (PCR), and Max Pain visualization for NIFTY, BANKNIFTY, and equity F&O contracts.
  • Real-Time Option Greeks: Dynamic computation of Delta, Gamma, Theta, and Vega with Implied Volatility (IV) and IV Percentile (IVP) curves.
  • Options Strategy Builder: Visual payoff diagrams for multi-leg strategies (Bull Call Spreads, Straddles, Iron Condors, Calendar Spreads).
  • Futures Build-up Heatmaps: Automated real-time classification of Long Build-up, Short Covering, Short Build-up, and Long Unwinding.
Phase 3 Autonomous Agentic AI & Portfolio Auditor
Proactive, autonomous portfolio intelligence optimizing risk and tax liability.
  • CAS Portfolio Health Auditor: Drag-and-drop parsing of NSDL/CDSL Consolidated Account Statements (CAS) to diagnose sector concentration risk and fund overlaps.
  • Automated Tax-Loss Harvesting: Proactive alerts prior to March 31 financial year-end identifying capital losses to offset STCG/LTCG tax liabilities under the Indian Income Tax Act.
  • Concall & Transcripts Intelligence: Automated AI ingestion of quarterly earnings calls; distills management guidance and margin headwinds into 60-second executive summaries.
  • Multilingual Voice & Chatbot: Regional Indian language support (Hindi, Gujarati, Tamil, Marathi, Bengali, Telugu).
Phase 4 IPO Central & Primary Market Intelligence
Comprehensive tracking of Mainboard and SME Initial Public Offerings.
  • Comprehensive IPO Radar: Track upcoming, open, and closed IPOs with price bands, issue sizes, lot quantities, and anchor investor allocations.
  • Grey Market Premium (GMP) Aggregator: Real-time estimated listing gains tracking.
  • Live Subscription Telemetry: Hourly bidding rates across QIB, NII (HNI), and Retail investor quotas.
  • AI DRHP Summarizer: Instant breakdown of Draft Red Herring Prospectus risk factors, promoter backgrounds, and proceeds utilization.
Phase 5 Omnichannel Delivery, Native Mobile & Institutional API
Cross-platform ecosystem expansion and B2B data monetization.
  • Native Mobile Applications: iOS & Android apps (React Native) with biometric authentication and lock-screen live activity tickers.
  • Omnichannel Alerts: Instant notifications delivered straight to WhatsApp and Telegram for target prices, corporate actions, and dividend dates.
  • Institutional Developer API: WebSocket L1/L2 data access, historical tick query endpoints, and white-label analytical widgets for fintech developers.
