1. Executive Summary & Vision

Dalal Street Pulse is a specialized, institutional-grade financial intelligence and analytics web application engineered specifically for the Indian equity ecosystem (NSE & BSE). Mainstream financial platforms often treat Indian markets as an afterthought, relying on synthetic US feeds, foreign currency units, or inaccurate session rules. Dalal Street Pulse addresses this by delivering native Rupee (₹) formatting with authentic Lakh/Crore notation, precise Asia/Kolkata (IST) trading session tracking, multi-tier data ingestion reconciling Dual Listings via ISIN, and grounded AI-driven equity valuations.

The Problem Solved Industry Pain Point
  • Generic tools lack Lakh/Crore grouping and IST trading session clocks (Pre-open, Muhurat, Normal, Post-closing).
  • Disconnected ticker systems that fail to reconcile dual-listed stocks (NSE vs BSE Scrip codes).
  • Superficial AI outputs disconnected from SEBI/exchange regulatory disclosures and fundamentals.
The Solution Provided Value Delivered
  • Zero synthetic data: verified NSE/BSE security masters (Mainboard & SME).
  • Grounded AI: fundamental health scoring & peer valuation multiple benchmarking.
  • Automated background workflows for price alerts and daily post-market briefings.

4. Detailed Future Scope & Product Roadmap

The roadmap below outlines the systematic transformation of Dalal Street Pulse from an intelligence portal into an all-in-one terminal for execution, derivatives analysis, algorithmic tooling, and primary market participation.

Phase 1 1-Click Broker Trading & Execution Gateway
Bridge the gap between market research and live execution by integrating directly with India's top retail stockbrokers via secure OAuth protocols.
  • Licensed Broker Connectors: Direct order routing via Zerodha Kite Connect, Groww, Upstox API, Angel One SmartAPI, and Dhan HQ.
  • 1-Click Order Execution: Place CNC (Cash & Carry), MIS (Intraday), and GTT (Good Till Triggered) orders directly from the stock details view.
  • Paper Trading Simulator: Virtual ₹10,00,000 INR account to allow novice investors to test algorithmic and discretionary strategies in real time without capital risk.
  • Basket & Thematic Orders: Execute multi-stock thematic baskets (e.g., "Top Nifty IT Leaders" or "High ROCE Green Energy") in a single confirmation.
Phase 2 NSE Derivatives (F&O) & Options Analytics Terminal
Deliver professional-grade tooling for India's high-volume index and equity options ecosystem.
  • Interactive NSE Option Chain: Live Open Interest (OI), Change in OI, Put-Call Ratio (PCR), and Max Pain visualization for NIFTY, BANKNIFTY, and equity F&O.
  • Real-Time Option Greeks: Dynamic computation of Delta, Gamma, Theta, and Vega with Implied Volatility (IV) and IV Percentile (IVP) curves.
  • Options Strategy Builder: Visual payoff diagrams for multi-leg option strategies (Bull Call Spreads, Straddles, Iron Condors, Calendar Spreads).
  • Futures Build-up Heatmaps: Automated classification of Long Build-up, Short Covering, Short Build-up, and Long Unwinding across all contracts.
Phase 3 Autonomous Agentic AI & Portfolio Auditor
Empower investors with personalized, autonomous agentic intelligence that monitors and optimizes their capital.
  • Consolidated Account Statement (CAS) Auditor: Drag-and-drop NSDL/CDSL CAS statement parsing to automatically audit portfolio risk, sector overweighting, and asset overlap.
  • Automated Tax-Loss Harvesting: Smart alerts before March 31 financial year-end identifying unrealized short-term and long-term capital losses to offset tax liabilities.
  • Concall & Transcripts Intelligence: AI audio/text ingestion of quarterly corporate conference calls to synthesize guidance, margin commentary, and management tone in 60-second summaries.
  • Multilingual Voice & Chatbot: Native support in regional languages (Hindi, Gujarati, Tamil, Marathi, Bengali, Telugu) for widespread financial literacy.
